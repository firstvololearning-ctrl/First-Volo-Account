Privacy requests

Parents and guardians can ask to review information, withdraw permission, request deletion or ask for a correction.

Choose an option below to prepare an email to us. You will send the request from your own email app. You can also email privacy@firstvololearning.com directly.

Keep your first message simple. Please do not send a child's name, student work, diagnosis, ID documents or login codes. We will reply with the next steps to verify your authority and find the appropriate record.

What happens next

We check privacy messages each business day. Before sharing or changing a learner's records, we take steps to verify your authority. We will explain the action taken and any remaining steps. Preparing an email here does not withdraw permission or delete records.

No parent permission is granted through this page.
