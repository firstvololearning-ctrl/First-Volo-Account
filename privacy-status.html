Privacy and security progress

Updated September 7, 2026

This status notice covers My First Volo, Story Builder, Primo Volo and First Volo Morpho (Morphology). It summarizes recorded implementation and testing. It is separate from the final Privacy Policy, which remains under review.

Current availability

Real parent invitations and new student access remain disabled. Setup invitations test email and response handling only. They do not establish parental consent or activate student access.

First Volo has implemented learner-deletion processing, access controls, failure alerts and encrypted record-delivery tools. Selected tests and an isolated database restoration have passed. Consent decisions, provider verification and remaining operational work are still open.

1. Protections in place

  • Account and record access: educator accounts and product permissions control the normal product experience. Database controls restrict records by authorized educator and learner context. The student-access design uses educator-issued codes, but the real-student release remains disabled.
  • Selected access tests: fictional-record checks covered record isolation and access denial after session revocation, entitlement expiry, class access removal and learner archiving. Representative browser journeys were also checked. These tests do not cover every route, role, device or session state.
  • Account security: Private Email two-factor sign-in was tested. The operator confirmed Gmail two-step verification and monitors the privacy and security inboxes. Leaked-password protection was enabled in the account service.
  • Setup email: the revised test invitation, response and confirmation reached the operator. A separate retention-notice test also reached the inbox. Neither test authorizes student use.
Use fictional details in evaluation activities. Choose made-up learner labels such as Owl 7. Keep real names, initials, school IDs, contact details, diagnoses and IEP documents out of labels, class names, stories and instructional notes. The fields do not automatically detect identifying text.

The products can save learning activity, work and progress linked to internal learner records. Omitting real names does not make those records anonymous. Authorized educators can review associated learning records. Browser storage and downloaded copies also need appropriate handling.

2. Parent and school authorization

A parent-consent pathway is being developed. The suitability of email-plus for the actual educator and service-provider access model remains unresolved. The final notice, verification method and release process have not been approved for real invitations.

Educator-issued codes establish access permissions, not consent. School use also requires the appropriate authorization and data arrangements. An educator account or a no-cost pilot does not by itself establish those arrangements. BPS approval and an executed district data agreement have not been established.

3. Security, retention and deletion

Ordinary learner deletion

The learner deletion-request feature is published. A request archives the learner and revokes linked student access. The ordinary workflow provides a 30-day cancellation period, followed by scheduled primary-database deletion checks. Fictional tests covered linked learning records and qualifying anonymous authentication accounts. Failure alerts were tested and their receipt confirmed.

This describes the ordinary account workflow, not a universal deadline for every parent or school request. The applicable request and agreement must be assessed. Removing primary records does not automatically remove mailbox messages, downloaded files, browser copies or every provider-held copy.

Product-specific retention

Product-specific deletion, journaling and restoration checks have been built and tested with fictional records. The operator evidence page is published and keeps cases on hold. Maintenance runs every 15 minutes to prepare eligible notices and check readiness. Automatic product-notice sending and product-specific deletion remain disabled.

General educator downloads, the independently verified backup receipt connection and remaining retention decisions are unfinished. One earlier scoped-deletion rehearsal failed; later runs passed, but the original cause has not been established.

Backups and restoration

An isolated database restoration test passed selected deletion and authentication checks, and the temporary project was removed. This was not a full recovery test of every service. Database backups do not include uploaded Storage objects.

Independent encrypted history now covers learner and product deletions. An initial downloaded backup copy matched the original and decrypted successfully. A later automated upload still awaits download verification. Ongoing unattended verification and its connection to product-deletion readiness remain unfinished. A check showing unchanged history is not proof of a fresh verified backup.

Parent record requests

Published operator tools prepare encrypted, scoped records and record delivery evidence. The operator still needs to verify the requestor's authority, deliver the file and its code separately, and complete the practical response process. Full fulfillment, correction and handling of remaining copies are not yet verified end to end.

Security program and providers

A written security program, incident procedures and a data/provider inventory have been prepared. Operational evidence, remaining provider assurances, technical logging review and some retention periods still need completion. First Volo has not completed an independent security audit.

4. COPPA progress

The FTC six-step compliance plan guides this review. The following are work statuses, not a certification.

  1. Collection review — partial. Focused product reviews exist; the complete field, runtime, identifier and provider inventory remains unfinished.
  2. Privacy policy — draft. A public-policy draft and separate private review checklist exist. Provider facts, retention and final wording remain under review.
  3. Direct notice — setup delivery tested. Test invitations and confirmations work. The final real-parent notice and delivery evidence flow remain unfinished.
  4. Consent — unresolved. Email-plus eligibility remains under review. Real invitations and student access remain disabled.
  5. Parent rights — tools published, fulfillment incomplete. Authority verification, review, withdrawal, correction and deletion require completion of the practical process.
  6. Security and retention — partly implemented. Ordinary learner deletion and alerts operate within the tested scope. Product automation, provider assurances and all-copy handling remain unfinished.

5. FERPA and school arrangements

School use requires a documented basis for handling education records and the applicable school control, access and disclosure arrangements. Technical safeguards alone do not establish those arrangements. See the Department of Education's guidance for service providers.

  • School authority and agreements: not established for a district release.
  • Access and correction: scoped tools exist; school-directed procedures and practical fulfillment need completion.
  • Return, retention and deletion: match verified operations and provider-copy handling to the applicable agreement.
  • BPS: application review, accepted integrations, institutional roles, accessibility evidence and applicable data agreements are separate remaining work.

Limits of this update

This page reports recorded checks as of its update date, not continuous verification of every safeguard. Legal review, additional security and accessibility review, final notices and some provider practices remain outstanding. No completed COPPA or FERPA compliance determination or district approval is claimed.

First Volo uses outside companies to host its programs, store information and deliver email. The final policy must accurately explain the companies involved and their handling of information. Static curriculum files, such as images and PDFs, are not confidential merely because the browser displays an account gate.

6. Questions and requests

For privacy questions or requests to review, correct or delete records, or withdraw permission, contact privacy@firstvololearning.com. For a suspected security issue, contact security@firstvololearning.com. Alexis Sacco monitors both addresses.

Please do not email passwords, sign-in codes or unnecessary student work. We will arrange appropriate verification and delivery before releasing records or carrying out sensitive requests.
