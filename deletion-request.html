Request deletion of one learner’s records across Story Builder, Primo, and Morphology.

  • The learner is removed from your active list and their current student sign-ins are disabled.
  • You have 30 days to cancel. Your request history shows the exact deadline in your local time.
  • After that deadline, automatic cleanup checks for due requests every five minutes. Failed attempts are retried and may delay completion. The status changes to Completed only when deletion has finished.
  • Deletion includes linked saved work and progress. Separately saved, unlinked profiles, downloaded copies, and backups need separate handling. Contact us about those copies.

Use the existing label to select the learner. Do not enter a real name or other personal information.
